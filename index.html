What we do

Monthly meter reading

Every unit's meter and the bulk supply meter, read on site on the same agreed date each month.

Photo record

A dated photo of every meter, kept on file and available on request.

Consumption report

Previous reading, current reading and units used for every unit, as a spreadsheet your agent can bill from and a PDF for the record.

Reconciliation

We check all the meters on the property against the bulk supply meter and report the difference. Common-property use can be shared by participation quota.

How it works

  1. 1

    We agree the dates

    The reading date and the report date are set around your billing cycle and stay the same every month.

  2. 2

    We read every meter on site

    Each meter is photographed as it is read.

  3. 3

    Every reading is checked

    Our software captures the reading from the photo, and a person confirms it against the photo before it is saved.

  4. 4

    Unusual readings are flagged

    Built-in checks catch a reading that is lower than last month's, a meter that has rolled over, and usage that is out of line.

  5. 5

    You receive the report

    It reaches you by the date we agreed, in time for your billing run.
